Creative Audit™

The Creative Audit™ is an independent strategic diagnostic engagement that assesses the quality, consistency, relevance and performance potential of an organisation's creative system. Its purpose: to identify blockages, reveal opportunities and define the priority creative decisions.

It is not an aesthetic critique of campaigns, but a way to understand why a creative system produces, or fails to produce, the expected results. Its distinctive value: analysing the link between brand strategy, creative intelligence, organisation and performance.

Understand your creative performance. Identify what needs to change.

01/ 05

What the audit includes

The client is not buying an opinion. They are buying a structured reading of their creative environment and the decisions to improve it.

  • 01

    Creative Intelligence Report™

    The main deliverable. A diagnostic report.

    • Analysis of brand positioning.
    • Analysis of identity and creative territory.
    • Analysis of existing campaigns and content.
    • Analysis of competition and differentiation.
    • Analysis of consistency between strategy and execution.
    • Identification of untapped opportunities.
  • 02

    Creative Quality Scorecard™

    An evaluation grid out of 100 points, built around seven dimensions.

    • Strategic Fit: strategic alignment.
    • Big Idea: strength of the idea.
    • Cultural Relevance: cultural relevance.
    • Distinctiveness: uniqueness.
    • Consistency: coherence.
    • Craft: quality of design.
    • Impact: potential impact.
  • 03

    Creative Performance Diagnosis

    Identification of the elements that explain current performance.

    • Structural strengths.
    • Creative weaknesses.
    • Strategic inconsistencies.
    • Organisational friction.
    • Risks of performance loss.
  • 04

    Priority Opportunities

    Three priority opportunities, ranked by potential impact and feasibility.

  • 05

    Strategic Action Plan

    A roadmap.

    • The decisions to make.
    • The priority recommendations.
    • The quick wins.
    • The workstreams to start.
    • The next strategic steps.
Expected outcome

The leader understands where their organisation stands, what holds back its creative performance and what to change first.

02/ 05

The five phase process

A cycle of 5 to 7 working days. A serious diagnosis cannot be done in a 90 minute meeting.

  • 01
    Day 1

    Phase 1 · Discovery & Scoping

    Scoping meeting with the decision maker.

    • Understand the company's ambitions.
    • Identify the perceived problems.
    • Define the scope of the audit.
    • Collect documents and materials.
  • 02
    Day 2

    Phase 2 · Stakeholder Interviews

    Targeted interviews with stakeholders, to understand how creative decisions are made and how teams work.

    • General management.
    • Marketing and communication management.
    • Brand manager.
    • Creative lead or partner agency, where relevant.
  • 03
    Days 3 and 4

    Phase 3 · Creative Intelligence Analysis

    In depth analysis.

    • Positioning and strategy.
    • Creative territory.
    • Campaigns and content.
    • Digital presence.
    • Competition.
    • Customer journey.
    • Creative organisation and processes.
  • 04
    Day 5

    Phase 4 · Strategic Synthesis

    Building the report, scoring, ranking the problems and formalising the recommendations.

  • 05
    Days 6 and 7

    Phase 5 · Executive Readout

    Readout to the decision makers, in 60 to 90 minutes.

    • Presentation of the diagnosis.
    • Discussion of the results.
    • Presentation of the three priorities.
    • Discussion of the options for intervention.

The readout is a decision meeting, not a simple presentation.

What we need before starting

Before starting, MAIORIS asks for the following information.

CompanyPresentation, sector, activities, markets
AmbitionsCommercial and marketing objectives over 6 to 12 months
BrandPositioning, identity, brand platform
CommunicationStrategy, campaigns, editorial calendar
CreationBriefs, visuals, videos, concepts, advertising
PerformanceIndicators, campaign results, available analytics
CompetitionMain competitors and references
OrganisationTeams, agencies, responsibilities, approval processes
IssuesDifficulties encountered and expectations of MAIORIS

The five particularly useful documents

  • Brand guidelines or brand platform.
  • Marketing or communication plan.
  • Three to five recent campaigns.
  • Available performance reports.
  • Organisation chart of the marketing and communication team.

Access to the people who make the decisions is necessary.

The client does not need to hold all of these documents to start. Missing information is identified during scoping and flagged as possible limits of the diagnosis.

05/ 05

Which organisations it is for

The Creative Audit™ is for organisations with enough marketing challenges, creative complexity and resources to benefit from a strategic diagnosis.

Established companies

Structured SMEs, mid sized firms and large groups with growth ambitions and communication investment.

Marketing departments

Organisations with a marketing or communication team but lacking a cross cutting creative vision.

Brands in transformation

Companies launching a new brand, changing positioning, entering a new market or preparing a new phase of growth.

Multi brand organisations

Groups managing several brands, markets, teams or agencies with consistency and creative governance challenges.

Who it is not for

  • Companies looking only for a graphic designer or a video editor.
  • Clients who want immediate advertising production.
  • Organisations with no identifiable marketing or creative issue.
  • Companies that only want aesthetic validation.
  • Clients who refuse to share the information needed for the diagnosis.
  • Organisations looking for guaranteed commercial results from a simple audit.
