§ 01 · Business challenge

Creative decisions are taken without a senior owner.

Marketing teams manage agencies, deadlines and budgets. Nobody has the time or the mandate to hold the creative vision and arbitrate quality over time.

§ 02 · Strategic opportunity

Better creative decisions, every month.

A dedicated creative leadership improves briefs, challenges agencies, protects the brand and connects creative work to business indicators.

§ 03 · Methodology

Govern

Creative governance: who decides what, and on which criteria.

Direct

Creative direction of campaigns and partners; production stays with your teams or partner studios.

Measure & improve

Monthly review of creative and operational performance.

§ 04 · Scope

Creative strategy, campaign leadership, agency governance, team development. Production and media buying budgets remain separate from fees.

Not included: creative production and media buying.

§ 05 · Deliverables

  • Monthly performance review

§ 06 · Engagement model

§ 07 · Expected outcomes

  • A senior owner for creative decisions.
  • Sharper briefs and more accountable partners.
  • Creative work connected to business indicators.

§ 08 · Why this offer

Creative Leadership as a Service is a recurring leadership engagement: an external senior creative leadership capability on an ongoing basis. It is not Creative Direction billed monthly. Creative Direction is a mission with a defined scope for a given initiative, campaign or project, with no automatic production responsibility.

§ 09 · The business case

Access senior creative leadership without necessarily building a permanent executive structure.

Three ways to obtain senior creative leadership, compared on what changes structurally. None is systematically cheaper than the others: the right choice depends on your scope, your priorities and how much leadership you really need.

When it is appropriate

  • You work with several agencies or vendors and nobody holds the creative direction.
  • Your need for senior creative leadership is real but does not justify a full time executive role.
  • Creative decisions on briefs, quality and priorities are taken without a senior owner.
  • You want a shared governance framework before deciding on a permanent structure.

Conditions that make it valuable

  • A decision maker who engages at the agreed rhythm.
  • Business priorities stated clearly enough to connect creative work to them.
  • A defined mandate given to the external leader.
  • Internal teams or partners who produce the work.

Who benefits

CEOs, marketing and communication directors of organisations that treat creativity as a strategic capability.

When it is not the right model

When the need is daily art direction, hierarchical management of a team or creative production: this engagement does not cover them.
