Level 02 · Fractional Creative Leadership

Fractional Creative Director

An outsourced creative direction engagement for organisations that need strategic creative leadership without necessarily hiring a full time creative director.

Pricing indicationFrom 2 500 000 FCFA / month

Discover Creative Leadership as a Service

Why this model exists

Many organisations need creative leadership without the role justifying a full time hire. This model provides access to strategic creative direction, coordination capability and organisational thinking, without creating an additional permanent department.

Who it is for

Organisations that see creativity as a strategic capability, and whose creative activities are becoming hard to coordinate, evaluate or align with their business priorities.

What it helps address

Several agencies without unified direction, inconsistent brand identity across channels, briefs that generate repeated revisions, no creative governance framework. The problem is not always a lack of creativity: it often lies in the absence of a system capable of directing it.

Areas of intervention
  • Strategic creative direction
  • Creative governance
  • Brand consistency
  • Coordination of creative resources
  • Structuring of decision processes
  • Improvement of creative performance
The value of outsourced direction

It does not sell production time or deliverables. It provides clear direction, a shared decision framework and better consistency between brand ambitions and their expression.

How the scope is defined

The scope, availability, duration and contractual terms are determined during a commercial qualification, according to your organisation and your challenges.
