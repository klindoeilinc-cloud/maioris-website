Advisory / Transformation 90

Ninety days to move from diagnosis to a system that runs.

An intensive programme that diagnoses, designs and puts in place the priority changes (strategy, creative system and the place of AI) with your teams.

Format
Programme with a fixed horizon
Duration
90 days
For
CEOs, marketing and communication directors
Pillar
Performance Systems
Investment

Request a strategic assessment

§ 01 · Business challenge

Change has been discussed for a long time, but has not happened.

Studies and recommendations exist. What is missing is the sequence, the leadership and the time to make them real inside the organisation.

§ 02 · Strategic opportunity

Visible change within a single quarter.

A fixed horizon creates momentum: decisions are taken, new routines are tested, and results are measured before the energy fades.

§ 03 · Methodology

Days 1 to 30

Observe, diagnose, prioritise.

Days 31 to 60

Design and govern: the target system and its rules.

Days 61 to 90

Execute, measure, adjust, hand over.

§ 04 · Scope

Strategy, creative system, AI adoption and the indicators that connect them.

Not included: creative production and media buying.

§ 05 · Deliverables

  • Diagnosis and priorities
  • Target creative operating system
  • AI use framework
  • Performance dashboard definition and handover

§ 06 · Engagement model

  • Programme with a fixed horizon
  • Run end to end by senior leadership
  • Can continue through CLaaS

§ 07 · Expected outcomes

  • Priority changes implemented, not just recommended.
  • Teams trained on the new routines.
  • A measured starting point for continuous improvement.
