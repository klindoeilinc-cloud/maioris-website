Agencies · Studios · Specialist partners

Agencies and partners.

MAIORIS does not seek to replace agencies. We believe organisations must first learn to better direct the skills they already have.

Our role is strategic direction, alignment and the improvement of creative performance. Production stays with the teams, agencies and studios that master it.

01/ 05

Three complementary functions

An effective creative ecosystem rests on three functions. They are complementary, but not interchangeable.

  • 01

    Strategic direction

    Defining what the organisation must communicate, why it matters and which priorities guide its creative activity.

  • 02

    Creative execution

    Translating strategic direction into campaigns, identities, content, experiences and other deliverables.

  • 03

    Creative governance

    Making sure the different contributors work within a consistent framework, with clearly defined responsibilities and evaluation mechanisms.

MAIORIS provides strategic direction and governance. Execution belongs to the agency or the studio.

02/ 05

How we work with agencies

Our aim is not to replace these players, but to enable organisations to better steer and make the most of their creative ecosystem.

  • We can work alongside internal teams, agencies, production studios and a range of specialist partners.
  • MAIORIS provides strategic creative direction and coordination capability.
  • Each engagement is led according to the MAIORIS methodological framework, with the skills suited to the needs of the project.
  • MAIORIS keeps its central role: strategic direction, alignment and the improvement of creative performance.

03/ 05

Two situations

Agencies and partners come to us in two kinds of situation.

Senior strategic direction

Our Creative Leadership as a Service™ offer is also available to agencies that need senior strategic direction.

Complementary expertise

We work with strategic agencies and specialist partners when projects call for complementary expertise.

04/ 05

What we do not do

We direct the work. We do not sell volume.

  • MAIORIS does not take on production.
  • No on demand graphic production.
  • No post by post community management.
  • No media buying or event logistics.
  • We do not seek to replace agencies.

05/ 05

The terms

The scope, roles and conditions of the collaboration are defined case by case, during a first conversation. No commercial terms are published at this stage.
