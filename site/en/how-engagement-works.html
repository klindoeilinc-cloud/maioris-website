Engagement journey

How engagement works. Five stages, from first contact to activation.

Every engagement follows the same path, whether you are a brand, a group or an agency. You know what to expect at each stage, what MAIORIS needs from you, and what happens next.

Good to know

This page describes how engagements typically unfold. The scope, duration, deliverables and fees of each mission are set in writing before work starts.

The five stages

  1. 01

    Initial Contact

    What happens

    You reach MAIORIS through the qualification form, by email, on WhatsApp, or by booking a conversation.

    What you can expect

    A person reads every request and replies by email to propose a first conversation.

    What we need from you

    Who you are, the situation you face and, if you already have one in mind, the offer that interests you.

    What happens next

    A first conversation is proposed.

  2. 02

    Strategic Discovery

    What happens

    A first conversation of about thirty minutes, without a sales presentation, to understand the decision you have to take.

    What you can expect

    Questions about your organisation, your ambitions and what you have already tried.

    What we need from you

    The decision maker, and the context: objectives, current organisation and recent work.

    What happens next

    MAIORIS assesses how it can help, and with which offer.

  3. 03

    Strategic Recommendation

    What happens

    MAIORIS recommends the offer and the scope that fit your situation, and the right entry point.

    What you can expect

    A reasoned recommendation, not a catalogue purchase.

    What we need from you

    Your feedback on the recommendation and, if useful, additional context or documents.

    What happens next

    If you wish to continue, a proposal is prepared.

  4. 04

    Proposal and Agreement

    What happens

    A written proposal sets out the scope, the deliverables, the duration and the fees. An agreement confirms the conditions.

    What you can expect

    The roles of each party, the boundaries of the engagement and what is excluded are made explicit.

    What we need from you

    Your validation of the proposal, and the people who sign for your organisation.

    What happens next

    Signature, then activation.

  5. 05

    Engagement and Activation

    What happens

    The mission starts with a kick off and a working rhythm that depends on the offer: a defined intervention, a programme with a fixed horizon, or a recurring leadership.

    What you can expect

    Reviews and recommendations at the moments set in the agreement.

    What we need from you

    Access to the people and materials listed in the proposal, and a decision maker available at the key moments.

    What happens next

    The agreed deliverables are delivered. A following step can then be discussed.

Agencies

Agencies follow the same path

The agency remains the owner of its client relationship. The collaboration model (A, B or C) and its boundaries are confirmed at the recommendation and agreement stages.
