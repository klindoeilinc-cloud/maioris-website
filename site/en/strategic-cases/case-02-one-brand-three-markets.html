02Context

A fictitious consumer goods company wants to accelerate its growth in West Africa. Present in several markets, it notices a growing number of local adaptations of its identity and its communications.

03Constraint

Reconciling local relevance and regional consistency.

04Strategic decision

Structure a brand governance system built on:

  • A common strategic foundation.
  • Territories of expression that adapt to local realities.
  • A decision framework that preserves brand consistency.

05Illustrative result

The new system aims to reduce inconsistencies between markets, make communications easier to approve and strengthen brand recognition.

06Proposed success indicators

  • Consistency of communications across markets.
  • Shorter approval cycles.
  • Adoption of the framework by local teams.

07Strategic lesson

Regional expansion must not multiply identities. It must intelligently organise their expression.

08The MAIORIS perspective

A strong regional identity distinguishes what must stay constant from what can adapt. This is the logic of creative governance that MAIORIS places at the heart of its approach: a common foundation, local expressions and a shared decision framework.
