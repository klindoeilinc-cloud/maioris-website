02Context

A fictitious organisation with an in house marketing team starts to integrate several artificial intelligence tools into its daily activities.

Team members experiment with different solutions to produce content, research ideas and speed up certain tasks.

03Constraint

Practices remain individual and scattered. The organisation risks multiplying tools without really improving how it works.

04Strategic decision

Design an AI augmented creative infrastructure:

  • Mapping of creative processes.
  • Identifying the relevant tasks to augment.
  • Defining usage rules.
  • Structuring AI assisted workflows.
  • Putting quality standards in place.
  • Defining performance indicators.

05Illustrative result

A targeted reduction in repetitive tasks, better traceability of processes and more consistent use of artificial intelligence.

06Proposed success indicators

  • Time spent on repetitive tasks.
  • Workflow adoption rate.
  • Compliance with brand standards.
  • Quality of assisted output.
  • Time reinvested in high strategic value activities.

07Strategic lesson

The real transformation through AI is not about multiplying tools. It is about rethinking the working system in which they sit.

08The MAIORIS perspective

MAIORIS approaches artificial intelligence as a component of a creative system, not as a set of tools. Map, set rules, measure: technology strengthens human capability when it sits within a framework.
