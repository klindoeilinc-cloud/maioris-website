Editorial

The creative economy is changing. Expectations of brands, organisations and their creative ecosystems are shifting.

MAIORIS Insights explores the major strategic questions that come with these changes.

From creative direction to brand intelligence, from organisational performance to artificial intelligence, and through the dynamics of African markets, our analyses question new ways of thinking, organising and valuing creativity.

More than a news space, MAIORIS Insights is a platform for strategic thinking, reasoned perspectives and action oriented analysis.
