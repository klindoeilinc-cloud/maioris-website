Assess your brief in 2 minutes.

Five questions drawn from our analysis of the cost of a vague brief. Your answers stay in your browser: nothing is sent or stored.

01The business problem

Does the brief name the business problem to be solved, and not only what has to be produced?

02The audience

Does it specify the perception, behaviour or decision you want to influence in a defined audience?

03The objective

Is the result the creative intervention must produce stated clearly?

04The strategic direction

Is there a proposition or guiding idea steering the work?

05The success criteria

Are the criteria for judging the result defined before execution?
