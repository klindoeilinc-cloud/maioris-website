Work / VeOne

From five days to two: organising creative production by priorities.

Marko Leo's work as Creative Motion Lead at VeOne in Abidjan, before founding MAIORIS.

Role
Creative Motion Lead
Period
March 2023 to June 2026
Discipline
Motion design

Context

VeOne produced motion design deliverables for brands including Moov (Côte d'Ivoire and Togo), OLA Energy and SanlamAllianz.

Contribution

Production was reorganised around explicit priorities and tooled working methods, with daily team schedules as the steering instrument.

Result

Average delivery time reduced from 5 days to 2.

Source: internal daily team schedules, VeOne.

What it taught us

Speed in creative work rarely comes from working harder. It comes from removing the constraint that makes everything wait.
