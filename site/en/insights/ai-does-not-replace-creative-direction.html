Insights / AI & creative systems

AI does not replace creative direction. It exposes its absence.

Producing faster has never been easier. Deciding better has not changed. How to give AI a place in a creative team without losing the brand.

Marko Leo19 November 20264 min read

Generative tools have changed the economics of production. A visual, a script, ten variations of a headline: what used to take days now takes minutes. Many teams have concluded that the question is which tool to adopt. It is the wrong starting point.

When production becomes almost free, the scarce resource moves. It is no longer the ability to make, but the ability to choose: which idea, for which audience, in which tone, with which level of risk. A team without clear creative direction does not become more efficient with AI. It produces more, faster, and drifts further from its brand.

The question is not which tool to adopt, but which decisions must remain human.

Four kinds of tasks

Before choosing any tool, we classify each task of the creative chain into four families. The exercise is simple, and it immediately shows where AI creates value and where it creates risk.

  • Automatable: resizing, formatting, transcriptions, first sorting of feedback.
  • AI-assisted: research, exploration of variants, drafts that a person will rewrite.
  • Human expertise: strategy, the big idea, cultural judgement, art direction.
  • Mandatory validation: anything that commits the brand, its reputation or its legal responsibility.

Architecture before tools

In West Africa, this classification has a particular weight. Language, humour, references and codes of representation vary from one city and one generation to another. A tool trained elsewhere does not know them. Cultural judgement is not a detail to correct at the end: it belongs in the human family, by design.

Once the families are defined, tools become an implementation question: replaceable, measurable, governed. That is what we call an AI creative infrastructure. It does not make creative direction optional. It makes it indispensable, and finally visible.
