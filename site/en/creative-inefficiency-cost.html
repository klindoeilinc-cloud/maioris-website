Plain words
Rework:
anything redone or corrected after a first delivery.
Approval:
the step where someone signs off or asks for changes.
Social charges:
the contributions an employer pays on top of salary.
Media:
buying space to broadcast your messages: TV, radio, billboards, paid social media.
FCFA:
franc of the African Financial Community.
How it is calculated
  • Rework on the budget: annual budget × share spent on rework.
  • Cost of one hour: monthly cost ÷ 173 hours (40 hours a week, 52 weeks, divided by 12 months).
  • Time spent: people × hours per week × cost of one hour × 48 working weeks.
  • Total: rework on the budget + time spent.
  • Scenarios: total × 10%, 20% or 30%.

Estimate based only on your answers. It does not account for the effect of delays on your sales or for opportunity cost.
