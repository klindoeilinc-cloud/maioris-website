02 / 09

Who we work with, and why they call us

Independent creative agencies, branding studios, communication, digital and advertising agencies, and brand consulting firms. Typically teams of 2 to 30 people.

Agencies and firms

  • Independent creative agencies
  • Branding studios
  • Communication agencies
  • Digital agencies
  • Advertising agencies
  • Brand consulting firms
  • Creative studios of 2 to 30 people

Priority markets: France, Côte d'Ivoire, Senegal, Canada and French speaking Africa.

Situations we are brought in for

  • A strategy that lives in the founder's head rather than in the organisation.
  • Briefs, validations and handovers that follow no shared framework, and rework as a consequence.
  • Growth that moves faster than the structure meant to carry it.
  • AI tools adopted one by one, without governance or quality control.
  • Founder decisions taken without a senior sounding board outside the agency.

06 / 09

How we work with agencies

Three collaboration models, depending on who the client is and where the strategic work sits. The scope of MAIORIS is always defined by contract.

A

Agency Advisory

MAIORIS supports the founder or the leadership of the agency directly.

The agency is MAIORIS's contractual client.

B

Strategic Co-consulting

MAIORIS works alongside an agency on a strategic mission for the agency's own client. The agency keeps the main commercial relationship, unless agreed otherwise.

MAIORIS acts within its contractually defined strategic scope.

C

Direct Corporate Engagement

MAIORIS supports an advertiser directly. A partner agency can take on creation and production needs.

The advertiser is MAIORIS's contractual client.

07 / 09

Who does what

MAIORIS strengthens the agency. It does not take its place, its clients or its production.

MAIORIS provides

  • Strategic intelligence
  • Creative leadership
  • Campaign architecture
  • Creative governance
  • Operating systems
  • AI transformation advisory

The agency keeps

  • Its client relationship
  • Commercial ownership of its accounts
  • Production and execution responsibilities
  • Its creative production teams
  • Final production delivery

MAIORIS does not become a production agency and does not compete with its agency partners. White label collaboration is possible only when explicitly agreed and defined by contract.
