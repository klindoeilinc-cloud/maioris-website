Privacy

Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and how you can control it.

Controller

MAIORIS Group, Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire. Contact: hello@maioris.net

Data collected

Through the contact form: organisation, role, sector, country, description of your challenge, type of support sought, optional budget range, name, work email and optional phone number. Nothing else is collected about you.

Purpose

To answer your request and prepare a first conversation. A confirmation email is sent to you automatically after you submit the form. Your data is not sold, rented or used for advertising.

Retention

Data sent through the form is kept for as long as needed to handle your request and any business relationship that follows, then deleted.

MAIORIS Insights newsletter

If you subscribe to the newsletter: email address and the language of the sign-up page. Purpose: to send you the monthly newsletter. Basis: your consent, given by ticking the box provided. You can unsubscribe at any time, using the link in every issue or by writing to hello@maioris.net. The address is kept until you unsubscribe. Sending is handled by the provider Resend.

Creative Operations Intelligence tool

The answers you enter in the tool are processed in your browser. They are neither sent nor saved, and disappear when you close the page. If, and only if, you choose to send your Snapshot to MAIORIS by ticking the consent box, we receive your answers, the calculated result, your name, your email address and, if you give it, your organisation. Purpose: to reply to you and prepare a first conversation. Basis: your consent. An acknowledgement is sent to you by email, delivered by the provider Resend. See the section “Retention of results you send”. The tool's result is an indicative estimate based on your declarations: it is neither verified nor guaranteed.

Creative Maturity Assessment and Brand Consistency Review tools

These two tools work like Creative Operations Intelligence: the calculation runs in your browser, nothing is sent or saved, and everything disappears when you close the page. In the Brand Consistency Review, the files you choose are only checked (format and size) and listed in this tab: they are not read, analysed or sent. If, and only if, you choose to send your result to MAIORIS by ticking the consent box, we receive your answers or the text of your report (findings, material names, links), your name, your email address and, if you give it, your organisation. No file is transmitted. Purpose: to reply to you and prepare a first conversation. Basis: your consent. An acknowledgement is sent to you by email, delivered by the provider Resend. See the section “Retention of results you send”.

Retention of results you send

When you send a result to MAIORIS with your consent, it is saved in a database hosted by Cloudflare: your profile (organisation, sector, size, market if you gave them, name and email address), the assessment (questionnaire version, answers, indicators), the text report and the exact wording of the consent you gave. We do not keep your IP address. Retention: 12 months from the date of sending, after which the content is erased automatically; only a technical row with no personal data remains. Access: restricted to authorised MAIORIS staff, behind authentication, with every view, export, deletion or purge recorded in a log. Deletion: at any time, from the “Your results in this tab” panel (button “Delete my data sent to MAIORIS”, available if you turned on keeping in this tab before sending) or by writing to hello@maioris.net. No document is received or kept at present. There is no public personal space: nobody can view an assessment just by knowing an address.

Shared profile across the tools

If you tick the box “Keep my profile and results in this tab”, a limited profile (organisation, sector, size, market) and a summary of each result are kept in your browser's session storage, so that you are not asked for the same information twice and your results can be consolidated. This is off by default, stays in this tab, is never sent automatically, disappears when the tab is closed and can be cleared at any time from the panel. This is not secure storage: do not enter sensitive information.

Audience measurement

We measure visits with a cookie-free, privacy-friendly tool that collects no personal data. No advertising or tracking cookie is set.

Booking

If you display the booking calendar, it is provided by Cal.com and only loads at your request. The information you enter there is processed by Cal.com under its own privacy policy.

Your rights

Under Ivorian law No. 2013-450 on the protection of personal data, you may access, correct or delete your data, or object to its processing, by writing to hello@maioris.net. You may also contact the ARTCI.
