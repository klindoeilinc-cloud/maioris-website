About

Built in Abidjan. Designed for what comes next.

MAIORIS takes its name from the Latin maior, meaning greater, beyond. It exists to give African organisations the strategic and creative intelligence that ambitious growth requires.

Vision

A reference for strategic creative leadership in Africa.

We want African organisations to treat creativity as a strategic asset: decided, structured and measured, at the level of the best international firms.

Philosophy

Intelligence precedes execution.

Strategy precedes production. Systems make performance repeatable. We would rather fix the constraint than add to the noise.

Positioning

Upstream of decisions.

MAIORIS is not a communication agency, a graphic studio, a generalist digital agency or a production company. It works at the intersection of strategy, creative intelligence, leadership, technology, AI and organisational performance.

The firm works with a network of trusted partners for production, data and technology, under MAIORIS's direction.

Leadership

Marko Leo

Founder · Creative Strategist & Creative Director

Marko Leo founded MAIORIS after nine years leading creative work in Abidjan, as a creative strategist and creative director. He has built brand territories, led creative teams and reorganised creative production around priorities and measurable methods.

In previous roles he has worked on brands including TCL, Moov (Côte d'Ivoire and Togo), OLA Energy, SanlamAllianz and ANSUT. He is the author of the method that became MAIORIS CPS™.

Marko Leo is the professional name of Noé Karamoko.
