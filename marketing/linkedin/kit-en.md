## A1a : Brief : l'idée forte

**Week 1, Tuesday** · 724 characters


A disappointing creative result is not always poor execution.

Very often, the problem starts much earlier: when the brief is written.

A brief is not a document that describes what to produce. It is a strategic instrument that turns a business challenge into clear creative direction.

When that translation fails, the whole process becomes vulnerable: repeated revisions, longer timelines, misaligned expectations.

The real question is not "are we producing enough content?".
It is "are we solving the right problem from the start?".

## A1b : Brief : les cinq éléments

**Week 2, Tuesday** · 691 characters


5 elements an effective brief must establish:

1. The business problem: what really needs to change?
2. The audience: which perception, behaviour or decision are we trying to influence?
3. The objective: what result should the creative intervention produce?
4. The strategic direction: which idea should guide the work?
5. The success criteria: how will the result be judged?

Is one missing? That is often where the back and forth begins.

These five elements turn the brief into a genuine decision framework.

## A3b : ROI créatif : quatre dimensions

**Week 6, Tuesday** · 743 characters


Creative performance can be read along four dimensions:

1. Strategic effectiveness: does the intervention answer the brand or business problem that was identified?
2. Brand effectiveness: does it strengthen recognition, differentiation, relevance or consistency?
3. Operational effectiveness: do resources and processes work well?
4. Business contribution: can we assess its contribution to qualified demand, conversion, acquisition, retention or revenue?

Not every initiative influences all of these indicators. The measurement framework must adapt to the objective of each.

## A3c : ROI créatif : mesurer dès le départ

**Week 6, Thursday** · 728 characters


The most common mistake: waiting until the end of a campaign to decide how to evaluate its results.

Performance criteria must be defined before execution. That means:

• linking the initial brief to measurable objectives;
• setting the relevant indicators;
• identifying the data needed for evaluation;
• distinguishing deliverables produced, results obtained and longer term effects.

The best creative investment is not the one that produces the most deliverables. It is the one that creates the most value in relation to the problem it was meant to solve.

## A4b : Identité et expression

**Week 8, Tuesday** · 661 characters


A strong regional brand distinguishes what must stay constant from what can evolve.

Brand identity is the strategic foundation: purpose, positioning, promise, values, distinctive characteristics.

Brand expression translates that identity into a local context: language, cultural references, storytelling, campaign formats, distribution choices, communication priorities.

The real challenge is not to choose between consistency and adaptation. It is to build a system that makes both possible.

## A5b : IA : cinq dimensions d'une infrastructure créative

**Week 10, Tuesday** · 780 characters


Beyond individual AI tools, the real opportunity is an integrated creative infrastructure. It rests on five dimensions:

Intelligence: collecting and interpreting information on markets, audiences and cultural shifts.
Strategy: turning insights into clear priorities.
Systems: establishing repeatable methods, standards and governance.
Technology: integrating the relevant tools into the operating environment.
Performance: assessing quality, effectiveness and impact.

The aim is not to automate creativity without discernment. It is to build a system in which technology strengthens human capability.
